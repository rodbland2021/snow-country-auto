## Our Brands

We work with trusted, proven brands across 4WD accessories, suspension,
tyres, electronics and diesel performance:

- **ARB** - 4WD accessories, protection and touring equipment
- **Ironman 4x4** - suspension and touring accessories, Australian-made
- **Old Man Emu (by ARB)** - integrated 4WD suspension upgrades
- **Fulcrum Suspension** - suspension packages featuring Bilstein, Sachs,
  Monroe and King Springs
- **Roadsafe 4WD** - steering and suspension components
- **Super Pro Bushes** - polyurethane suspension bushings
- **Drivetech 4x4** - 4WD hard parts and accessories, including Sumo
  Springs load-assist suspension
- **Warn** - winches and recovery equipment
- **Safari** - snorkels and 4WD performance systems
- **Hayman Reese** - towing solutions and weight distribution systems
- **Redarc** - towing and 12V electrical equipment
- **KORR Lighting** - LED lighting for camping, 4WD and marine
- **GME** - UHF radios and communications equipment
- **Polaris Systems** - reversing cameras, dash cams and in-car
  electronics
- **Sakura Filtration** - automotive and heavy equipment filters
- **Companion Brands** - camping, touring and outdoor equipment
- **Torqit Performance** - diesel power modules, exhausts and throttle
  response upgrades (see also our Diesel Tuning page)
- **Super Mini Booster** - portable jump starters

**Tyres:** Kumho, Bridgestone, Federal, Mastercraft, Continental, GT
Radial, Toyo, and Mickey Thompson.
