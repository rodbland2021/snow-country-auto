## 4WD Specialist Centre

The Snowy Mountains demand a lot from a vehicle - alpine cold, steep
climbs, heavy towing and long distances. Snow Country Auto is a
complete 4WD specialist centre, preparing vehicles for touring, towing,
off-road travel and everyday alpine driving.

**Suspension & Lift Kits**
Fully integrated suspension upgrades for load-carrying, towing and
off-road control - from bolt-in shock/spring kits through to complete
lift kit packages, matched to how you actually use your vehicle.

**Bull Bars & Protection**
Bull bars, rear protection tow bars, side rails and underbody
protection, engineered for the specific fit of your vehicle rather than
a universal-fit compromise.

**Towing**
Tow bar fitment and towing solutions for cars, utes, vans and 4WDs,
including weight distribution systems and sway control for safe towing
through mountain roads. Wiring looms, trailer plugs and brake
controllers set up properly, not just bolted on.

**Recovery & Touring Gear**
Winches, recovery equipment, snorkels, dual battery systems, long-range
fuel tanks, and canopies/drawers/cargo barriers for genuine touring
setups.

**Driving Lights & Electrical**
Driving lights and light bars, reversing cameras, UHF radios and
electric brake controllers, fitted and wired in properly.

**4WD Tyres & Wheel Alignment**
A full range of 4WD tyre brands to suit your vehicle and how you drive -
plus wheel alignment and balancing on every service, so tyres wear
evenly and your vehicle tracks straight on and off road.

**Camping & Touring Accessories**
Awnings, roof top tents, fridges/freezers, roof racks and toolboxes for
the fully kitted-out tourer.
