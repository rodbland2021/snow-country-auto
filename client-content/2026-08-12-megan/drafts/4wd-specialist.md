## 4WD Specialist Centre

The Snowy Mountains demand a lot from a vehicle - alpine cold, steep
climbs, heavy towing and long distances. Snow Country Auto is a
complete 4WD specialist centre, preparing vehicles for touring, towing,
off-road travel and everyday alpine driving.

**Suspension & Lift Kits**
Fully integrated suspension upgrades for load-carrying, towing and
off-road control, featuring Old Man Emu by ARB, Ironman 4x4, Fulcrum
Suspension (Bilstein, Sachs, Monroe and King Springs), Roadsafe 4WD,
Super Pro Bushes, and Drivetech 4x4 Sumo Springs load-assist suspension.

**Bull Bars & Protection**
Bull bars, rear protection tow bars, side rails and underbody
protection from ARB and Ironman 4x4, engineered for the specific fit of
your vehicle rather than a universal-fit compromise.

**Towing**
Tow bar fitment and towing solutions from ARB, Ironman 4x4, Hayman
Reese and Redarc for cars, utes, vans and 4WDs, including weight
distribution systems and sway control for safe towing through mountain
roads. Wiring looms, trailer plugs and brake controllers set up
properly, not just bolted on.

**Recovery & Touring Gear**
Warn winches, recovery equipment, Safari snorkels, dual battery
systems, long-range fuel tanks, and Companion Brands camping gear for
genuine touring setups.

**Driving Lights & Electrical**
KORR Lighting driving lights and light bars, Polaris Systems reversing
cameras and dash cams, GME UHF radios, and electric brake controllers,
fitted and wired in properly.

**4WD Tyres & Wheel Alignment**
Kumho, Bridgestone, Federal, Mastercraft, Continental, GT Radial, Toyo
and Mickey Thompson tyres, matched to your vehicle and how you drive -
plus wheel alignment and balancing on every service, so tyres wear
evenly and your vehicle tracks straight on and off road.

**Filtration & Maintenance**
Sakura Filtration filters and the Super Mini Booster portable jump
starter, for keeping your 4WD reliable between services.

**Camping & Touring Accessories**
Awnings, roof top tents, fridges/freezers, roof racks and toolboxes from
Companion Brands and other leading suppliers for the fully kitted-out
tourer.
